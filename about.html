About NMDC Limited

A premier Public Sector Undertaking under the Ministry of Steel, driving India's mineral security and industrial growth for over six decades.

Company Overview

India's Largest Iron Ore Producer

NMDC Limited is a Navratna Central Public Sector Enterprise under the Ministry of Steel, incorporated in 1958, and is India's largest iron ore producer. It also operates India's only mechanized diamond mine at Panna, Madhya Pradesh. Its major iron ore production units are the Bailadila Complexes (Bacheli and Kirandul) in Dantewada district, Chhattisgarh, and the Donimalai/Kumaraswamy mines in Karnataka.

This page and internal CS&IT portal represent the Bacheli Complex unit specifically — highlighting its operational scale, high-grade iron ore extraction, digital infrastructure, e-procurement, and technology initiatives.

History & Legacy

From its incorporation in 1958 to becoming India's premier iron ore producer, NMDC's journey highlights pioneering mechanization, strategic mineral development, and corporate excellence across six decades.

1958

Incorporation

Incorporated as National Mineral Development Corporation under the Government of India.

1966

Diamond Mining Expansion

Acquired the Panna Diamond Project at Majhgawan, Madhya Pradesh — India's only mechanized diamond mine.

1968

First Mechanized Iron Ore Mine

Commissioned Bailadila Deposit-14 at Kirandul Complex, establishing India's first fully mechanized iron ore mine.

1977

Bacheli & Donimalai Commissioning

Commissioned Bailadila Deposit-5 (Bacheli Complex) in Chhattisgarh and the Donimalai mine in Karnataka.

1998

Miniratna Status

Conferred Miniratna Category-I status by the Government of India for financial and operational performance.

2008

Navratna Status

Conferred Navratna status, granting elevated financial autonomy and strategic decision-making powers.

Present

Vision 2030 & Diversification

Pursuing 100 MT annual iron ore production capacity by 2030, diversifying into steelmaking (NMDC Steel Limited, Nagarnar) and gold mining (Australia).

Future Growth Vision

Envisioning 100 MT Mining Capacity by 2030

NMDC's strategic roadmap focuses on tripling iron ore extraction capacity through brownfield mine expansions, greenfield project development, and advanced mechanization. Key growth pillars include AI-driven geological exploration, integrated slurry pipelines and logistics corridors, renewable energy adoption at mine sites, and a fully digitized operational backbone managed by the CS&IT Department.
