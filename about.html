About Bharat Minerals Limited

A premier Public Sector Undertaking under the Ministry of Mines, driving India's mineral security and industrial growth for over six decades.

Company Overview

India's Strategic Mining Enterprise

Bharat Minerals Limited (BML) is a Navratna-category Public Sector Undertaking engaged in the exploration, extraction, and processing of iron ore, bauxite, and coal across 12 operational mine sites spanning six states. Established to secure India's mineral self-reliance, BML supplies critical raw materials to the steel, aluminium, and power sectors.

With an annual turnover exceeding ₹4,500 crore and a workforce of 8,500+ professionals, BML combines deep geological expertise with modern mechanization, environmental stewardship, and digital innovation to deliver sustainable mining outcomes aligned with national development priorities.

History & Legacy

From a single iron ore mine in Karnataka to a multi-mineral mining conglomerate, BML's journey reflects India's industrial evolution and the nation's commitment to harnessing its mineral wealth responsibly.

1958

Foundation

Incorporated as a Government of India enterprise to develop India's first large-scale iron ore mining operations in the Bellary-Hospet belt.

1975

Multi-Mineral Expansion

Commenced bauxite mining in the Eastern Ghats, establishing supply chains to India's emerging aluminium industry.

2005

Navratna Status

Awarded Navratna recognition for operational excellence, financial performance, and strategic importance to the national economy.

2018

Digital Transformation

Launched enterprise-wide ERP integration, fiber network backbone, and smart mining technologies across all operational sites.

2026

Vision 2030 Roadmap

Charting a path to 100 MT annual mining capacity through sustainable expansion, AI-driven exploration, and zero-harm safety culture.

Future Growth Vision

Envisioning 100 MT Mining Capacity by 2030

BML's strategic roadmap focuses on tripling extraction capacity through brownfield mine expansions, greenfield project development, and advanced mechanization. Key growth pillars include AI-driven geological exploration, integrated logistics corridors, renewable energy adoption at mine sites, and a fully digitized operational backbone managed by the CS&IT Department.
