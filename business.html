Advanced Exploration Technology

BML leverages cutting-edge geological sciences and AI-driven analysis to identify, evaluate, and develop India's next-generation mineral reserves.

🛰️

Satellite Remote Sensing

Multi-spectral LISS-IV and Cartosat imagery for lithological mapping, structural delineation, and mineral alteration zone identification across 18,000 sq. km of exploration blocks.

🗺️

GIS Mapping & Modelling

Enterprise-grade ArcGIS and QGIS platforms for 3D geological block modelling, mine planning, reserves estimation, and environmental sensitivity overlays.

🔬

NABL-Accredited Labs

Two NABL-accredited central laboratories in Bhubaneswar and Ranchi conducting XRF, XRD, AAS, and wet chemical analyses for quality assurance and export certification.
