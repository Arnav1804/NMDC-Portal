Operations & Mineral Spectrum

NMDC Limited extracts and processes high-grade iron ore and natural diamonds across premier production complexes, driving India's steel sector self-reliance and industrial growth.

🪨

Iron Ore (Core Commodity)

High-grade hematite iron ore extracted from the world-class Bailadila Complexes (Bacheli and Kirandul) in Chhattisgarh and the Donimalai and Kumaraswamy mines in Bellary-Hospet, Karnataka. Serves as the primary high-Fe feedstock for India's domestic and export steel manufacturing sector.

Diamond

Sourced from the Majhgawan Kimberlite Pipe at Panna, Madhya Pradesh — India's only mechanized diamond mine. Operates under strict environmental standards with automated heavy media separation and optical sorting.

Advanced Exploration Technology

NMDC leverages cutting-edge geological sciences and AI-driven analysis to identify, evaluate, and develop India's next-generation mineral reserves.

🛰️

Satellite Remote Sensing

Multi-spectral LISS-IV and Cartosat imagery for lithological mapping, structural delineation, and mineral alteration zone identification across 18,000 sq. km of exploration blocks.

🗺️

GIS Mapping & Modelling

Enterprise-grade ArcGIS and QGIS platforms for 3D geological block modelling, mine planning, reserves estimation, and environmental sensitivity overlays.

🔬

NABL-Accredited Labs

Two NABL-accredited central laboratories in Bhubaneswar and Ranchi conducting XRF, XRD, AAS, and wet chemical analyses for quality assurance and export certification.
