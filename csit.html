Computer Systems & Information Technology

The CS&IT Department is the digital backbone of Bharat Minerals Limited — enabling secure, reliable, and scalable technology services across all mine sites, corporate offices, and operational facilities nationwide.

Department Overview

Powering BML's Digital Operations

With a team of 40+ IT professionals organized into five specialized units, the CS&IT Department manages enterprise infrastructure spanning 12 mine sites, 3 regional offices, and the corporate headquarters in New Delhi. The department ensures 99.9% network uptime, maintains 120+ physical and virtual servers, and supports over 8,500 end users.

From core network operations and data center management to ERP administration and cyber security, CS&IT delivers mission-critical services that keep BML's mining operations connected, compliant, and competitive in an increasingly digital industry landscape.

Our Services

Comprehensive IT services supporting every facet of BML's operational ecosystem.

🌐

Network Operations

Design, deployment, and 24×7 monitoring of enterprise LAN/WAN infrastructure across all BML locations. Manages 450+ network devices including core switches, routers, firewalls, and wireless access points on a unified fiber backbone.

99.9% Uptime SLA
🖥️

Server Administration

Provisioning, patching, and lifecycle management of 120+ physical and virtual servers hosting ERP, email, file services, and application workloads. Implements disaster recovery with RPO < 4 hours across dual data centers.

Dual DC Architecture
📊

ERP Support

End-to-end administration of SAP S/4HANA modules covering Finance, Materials Management, HR, and Plant Maintenance. Provides functional support, custom development, and integration with mine production systems.

SAP S/4HANA Live
🎧

Helpdesk Services

Tier-1 and Tier-2 IT support for 8,500+ users via phone, email, and self-service portal. Average resolution time under 4 hours for priority incidents with SLA tracking and escalation workflows.

12×6 Support Coverage
🔒

Cyber Security

SOC operations, vulnerability assessments, penetration testing, and compliance with CERT-In advisories. Deploys next-gen firewalls, SIEM, endpoint protection, and conducts quarterly security awareness training for all staff.

ISO 27001 Aligned
📡

Infrastructure Monitoring

Real-time monitoring of network, server, storage, and application health using enterprise NMS and APM tools. Proactive alerting, capacity planning, and performance optimization across the entire IT estate.

24×7 NOC Operations
