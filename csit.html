Computer Systems & Information Technology

The CS&IT Department is the digital backbone of the Bacheli Complex — enabling secure, reliable, and scalable technology services across the administrative building, township, screening plants, and mine sites (Bailadila Deposit-5 and Deposit-10/11A), with seamless connectivity to NMDC's corporate data center in Hyderabad.

Department Overview

Powering Bacheli's Digital Operations

With a dedicated team of IT professionals organized into specialized units, the Bacheli CS&IT Department manages local ICT infrastructure spanning the administrative building, mining office complexes, loading plants, and township facilities at Bacheli. The department ensures 99.9% network uptime, maintains local server racks and network nodes, and connects to NMDC's primary corporate data center in Hyderabad and DR site at Kirandul Complex.

From core network operations and local server management to ERP support and cyber security compliance, CS&IT delivers mission-critical services that keep Bacheli's iron ore mining operations connected, compliant, and competitive in a modern digital industry landscape.

Our Services

Comprehensive IT services supporting every facet of NMDC's operational ecosystem.

🌐

Network Operations

Design, deployment, and 24×7 monitoring of local LAN/WAN infrastructure across all Bacheli Complex facilities. Manages local core switches, routers, firewalls, and outdoor wireless access points connected via high-speed optical fiber backbone to Hyderabad HQ.

99.9% Uptime SLA
🖥️

Server Administration

Provisioning, patching, and lifecycle management of local server infrastructure supporting plant telemetry, CCTV storage, and local application workloads, seamlessly synced with NMDC's primary corporate data center in Hyderabad and DR site at Kirandul Complex.

Dual DC Architecture
📊

ERP Support

End-to-end administration of SAP S/4HANA modules covering Finance, Materials Management, HR, and Plant Maintenance. Provides functional support, custom development, and integration with mine production systems.

SAP S/4HANA Live
🎧

Helpdesk Services

Tier-1 and Tier-2 IT support for 8,500+ users via phone, email, and self-service portal. Average resolution time under 4 hours for priority incidents with SLA tracking and escalation workflows.

12×6 Support Coverage
🔒

Cyber Security

SOC operations, vulnerability assessments, penetration testing, and compliance with CERT-In advisories. Deploys next-gen firewalls, SIEM, endpoint protection, and conducts quarterly security awareness training for all staff.

ISO 27001 Aligned
📡

Infrastructure Monitoring

Real-time monitoring of network, server, storage, and application health using enterprise NMS and APM tools. Proactive alerting, capacity planning, and performance optimization across the entire IT estate.

24×7 NOC Operations
